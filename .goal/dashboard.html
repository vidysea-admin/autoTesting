AutoTester

A reusable AI testing platform where an operator submits any project URL, domain-scoped test-account credential references, and optional evals, conditions, use cases, video, audio, documents, text, email or Google Drive sources. AutoTester learns from supplied evidence or logs in and performs bounded breadth-first exploration, preserves a durable Portal Persona, compiles traceable best/worst/edge evals, reruns them after developer releases in a real visible browser, and reports workflow/API regressions through clear HTML, Excel, text and screenshots. First acceptance targets: Vidysea ERP and Pathlynks.
