## OBSERVATIONS (non-blocking; checker/maker to rule)

a. **G2 wording is wider than the behaviour.** "never changes ... a stop reason" holds for unbounded crawls (63/63 shapes equal), but under a binding bound reorder legitimately changes what is reached first: with `max_actions=6` under TEST_ACCOUNT, 3 of 7 providers finished `frontier empty` where the guidance-off crawl stopped at `max_actions` (`adv3.py` output). That is honest reordering, not a defect, but G2 should be amended to say "with no bound binding" so a later checker does not read it as a regression.
b. **Per-call cap ignores the remaining crawl wall clock** (`explore_guidance.py:183` `cap = min(per-call, total - model_s)`; `explore.py:59` is the only wall-clock check, between actions). A call started at 59 s of a 60 s wall clock may run up to `model_call_timeout_s` (20 s default) past it. Counted, but not clamped; consider `min(cap, wall_clock_s - elapsed)`.
c. **Model-tag part of the reuse key is membership, not equality** (`explore_guidance.py:240` `note.model in self.models`): a ranking stored by tier 2 (`glm`) is replayed while tier 1 (`deepseek`) would be tried first. Both are pinned, so low risk; say so in the contract or key on the tier order.
d. **`Crawl.provider` records a replayed tag as served** (`explore_guidance.py:242`): a crawl that made 0 calls reads `provider=<tag>`. `Crawl.guidance.calls`/`kg_hits` disambiguate, so low.
e. **Residual outside this unit:** the observer cuts names at 60 chars before any scrub (`enumerate.js:67` `.slice(0,60)`), so a secret straddling that cut reaches the prefix filter as a non-matching fragment. Pre-existing, same for every crawl artifact, not fixable in this module.
f. **Edge:** `model_call_timeout_s` and `model_total_s` both `inf` pass the schema, then `attempt_budget` raises `ValueError` (`providers/attempts.py:28`), which is counted as a strike and opens the breaker with the misleading reason `schema or call error (ValueError)`. Reject non-finite at the schema.
g. **Process:** nested `attempt_budget(2, ...)` under the chain's `attempt_budget(2*len(tiers), ...)` means tier 2 only gets one attempt and no repair, and GLM's measured 48 s cold start exceeds the 20 s default, so tier 2 is mostly theoretical until G12 measures real latency. Not a defect; a G12 item.
