## Open-question answers
- Q1 (where the annotation lives): additive `GraphNode.guidance` on `PortalPersona.graph` (`schema/portal_persona.py:215`), default None, omitted from JSON when unset.
- Q2 (where the crawl summary lives): `Crawl.guidance: CrawlGuidance | None` (`schema/crawl.py:263`).
- Bounds are `int|float|None`, not `int=0`. Reason: `filestore.write_json` uses `exclude_none`, so a None field leaves an old `crawl.json` byte-identical (G5); `0` would have serialised a new key. Defaults (20 s per call, 120 s total) are applied in `explore_guidance.limits`. This is my reading of G5/G6, for the checker to confirm.
- `RunApproval` signs actions, probes and wall_clock_s only, not `CrawlBounds`, so new bounds fields do not change signed bytes (pinned by `test_a_pre_change_signed_approval_row_still_verifies` against `tests/fixtures/crawl_guidance/pre_change_approval.jsonl`).
- D-082 item 1: two new stages modules, both with one-job docstrings: `stages/explore_guidance.py` (the call, bounds, fallback, KG reuse) and `stages/explore_guidance_payload.py` (pure: redacted payload, permutation, reorder). No helper was moved out of an existing module.
