## Why this needle family is distinct from AT-598's two

- `_double_b64_needles` double-encodes the secret's **UTF-8** bytes (base64 of base64); it never
  touches UTF-16.
- `_utf16_hex_needles` hex-encodes the UTF-16 bytes; it never base64-encodes them.
- Neither reaches "base64 of UTF-16 bytes" — the exact PowerShell `-EncodedCommand` wire format —
  which is why the checker's adversarial probe still found 8/8 misses even after both AT-598 needle
  families landed.
